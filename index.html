How to Play

  • Use ⬅️ / ➡️ or A / D to steer.
  • Hold ⬆️ or W for a burst of speed.
  • Drive smoothly to build skill.
  • Hitting a cone drops your skill.
  • Every 100 skill points upgrades your car.
